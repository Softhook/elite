
# Design Statement: Hermeneutic In-Game News Reporting Engine (The Galactic Echo)

This document outlines the design principles for the **Galactic Echo**, an in-game news generation system. The system's primary function is to transcend simple event reporting and serve as the player's primary interface for **interpreting the game's underlying Materialist Ontology**—where all conflict is a symptomatic expression of the battle for resource control and scarcity.

## I. Interpretive Focus and Editorial Mandate

The Galactic Echo adopts a rigorous **hermeneutic editorial stance**. All reported events must be interpreted through the lens of material causation. The news reports do not focus on *who* did *what*, but on **what material interest** was served or obstructed.

* **Core Mandate:** Translate political rhetoric, military action, and piracy into direct commentary on the flux of **resource ownership, scarcity, and distribution pathways**.
* **Example Transformation:**
    > **Shallow Report:** "Imperial Forces clash with Separatists over the Jupiter-6 Colony."
    >
    > **Hermeneutic Report:** "Scarcity-Driven Clash: Imperial Third Fleet moves to re-secure **Rare Earth Mineral Flow** from Jupiter-6, classifying Separatist autonomy bids as an illegal seizure of vital supply chain assets."

## II. Mapping Events to Philosophical Tensions

The system utilizes a structured matrix to map game events onto the resource-based ontology, ensuring the narrative always reinforces the Materialist framework.

| Game Event | Underlying Tension | News Interpretation/Theme |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Successful Pirate Raid** (Target: Hauler) | Order vs. Chaos / Materialist Parasitism | **Focus:** The structural failure of the current resource control regime to secure capital flow. Report emphasizes commodity loss and subsequent market anxiety. |
| **Resource Price Shock** (E.g., $R_A$ doubles) | Exploitation vs. Sustainability / Inherent Scarcity | **Focus:** Interpreted as a crisis signaling the systemic exhaustion of a resource sector. Report may feature interviews with miners/farmers stressing material limits. |
| **Major Police Action** (Victory) | Status Quo Defense / Imperial Superstructure | **Focus:** The successful reinforcement of established *property rights* and the prioritization of Imperial-sanctioned resource ownership. Reports celebrate the maintenance of "stable flow." |
| **Post-Human Interaction** (Diplomacy/Trade) | Materialism vs. Transcendence | **Focus:** Reports must critically question the *material independence* of the Post-Human Systems. Is their 'freedom' a mere illusion of material self-sufficiency, or a new resource arbitrage strategy? |

## III. Hermeneutic Style and Factional Bias

To reflect the ideological superstructure built upon the material base, the news engine will generate reports from at least three distinct, biased perspectives. While the core **material event** remains the same, the **interpretation** reflects the faction's resource goals.

1.  **Imperial State Media (The Core Echo):** *Tone: Formal, authoritative.* **Interpretation:** Events are framed as successes for **Order, Efficiency, and the unified control** necessary for stable resource production. Piracy is described as *anarchy* threatening vital supply.
2.  **Separatist Underground Feed (The Free Flow Channel):** *Tone: Agitative, populist.* **Interpretation:** Events are framed as resistance against **Imperial Exploitation and Resource Monopoly**. Piracy is often recast as a *necessary privateering action* against oppressive resource control.
3.  **Independent Hauler's Blog (The Freight Log):** *Tone: Pragmatic, commodity-focused.* **Interpretation:** Events are strictly measured by their immediate **impact on profit margins, fuel costs, and transit risk**. A clash is not political, but an expensive "supply chain disruption."

## IV. Procedural Generation Steps

The Galactic Echo follows a process-oriented pipeline to ensure interpretive depth in every report:

1.  **Game Event Trigger:** (e.g., A Hauler is destroyed; a Refinery is commissioned.)
2.  **Resource Impact Calculation:** Quantify the event's direct and projected effect on key resource scarcity metrics (e.g., $R_M$ supply down $10\%$).
3.  **Application of Materialist Core:** The system identifies the **underlying resource conflict** tied to the event (e.g., $R_M$ scarcity fuels Military action).
4.  **Application of Factional Interpretation Filter:** The core narrative is spun into three distinct reports using the assigned tone and bias.
5.  **News Report Output:** The final text is presented to the player, functioning as a persistent textual layer for **interpreting the game's ontological structure**.
